Estimated daily energy need
— kcal
Basal metabolic rate—
The calories your body burns just to stay alive — breathing, heartbeat, digestion, keeping your organs running — even if you stayed in bed all day and didn't move.
Activity adjustment—
Extra calories burned from moving around — walking, chores, workouts — on top of the basal rate above. The more active you told us you are, the bigger this number.
Goal adjustment—
Calories added or subtracted on top of what keeps your weight steady, sized for slow and sustainable change — not a crash diet or an aggressive surplus.
Calculated with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and standard activity multipliers. This is a population-level estimate, not a medical measurement — actual needs vary by 10–15%.

Medical report reader

Upload a lab report to see values compared against standard reference ranges.

How this works, and what it will never do This tool reads values that are explicitly present in your report and compares them only to published lab reference ranges. It does not diagnose, does not infer conditions you haven't been tested for, and does not recommend treatment. It will not guess at a value that isn't in the document. Always review results with your doctor.
