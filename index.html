— kcal
Basal metabolic rate—
The calories your body burns just to stay alive — breathing, heartbeat, digestion, keeping your organs running — even if you stayed in bed all day and didn't move.
Activity adjustment—
Extra calories burned from moving around — walking, chores, workouts — on top of the basal rate above. The more active you told us you are, the bigger this number.
Goal adjustment—
Calories added or subtracted on top of what keeps your weight steady, sized for slow and sustainable change — not a crash diet or an aggressive surplus.
Calculated with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and standard activity multipliers. This is a population-level estimate, not a medical measurement — actual needs vary by 10–15%.

Medical report reader

Upload a lab report to see values compared against the reference ranges printed on your own report.

How this works, and what it will never do This tool reads values that are explicitly present in your report and compares them only to reference ranges printed on that same report — never a general range from elsewhere. It does not diagnose, does not infer conditions you haven't been tested for, and does not recommend treatment. Always review results with your doctor.

How your markers are trending

What this is, and isn't These charts plot the numbers already printed on your saved reports, grouped by name. They show direction and position relative to your most recent report's printed range — nothing here judges whether a change is good, bad, or worth worrying about. Reports from different labs can use different reference ranges or test methods, so an apparent change can reflect that rather than a real change in you. Always discuss trends with your doctor.
Save at least one report with readable values to see trends here.

Notes for your dietitian

Suggestions to raise, not instructions to follow These are talking points, phrased the way a note to your dietitian might read — based on values from your reports that fall outside their printed range and have an established, general connection to diet. This app isn't deciding anything for you, and it doesn't flag every out-of-range value — only the ones with a real dietary angle worth raising, like blood sugar, lipids, or specific nutrients.

Doctor visits

Keep a record of your visits, and store prescriptions, consultation notes, and bills alongside them.

Storage, not interpretation This app never attempts to read or summarize what's written on a prescription or consultation note — handwriting is hard to read reliably, and medicine names vary too much by brand and region to guess safely. Everything searchable here (doctor, notes, medicines) is typed in by you. Files are kept as your own private, secure record.
